⚔ Age of Empires JS

Build your economy, advance through four Ages, raise an army and destroy the red enemy's base before they destroy yours.

Controls
Left click select  ·  drag box-select  ·  Right click move / gather / attack / build
W A S D / arrows / screen edge — pan camera  ·  wheel zoom
Shift+1-9 set control group  ·  1-9 recall (double-tap centers)  ·  Esc cancel placement
A attack-move  ·  M select army  ·  P pause  ·  Shift+command queues orders  ·  double-click selects same type
H jump to Town Center  ·  . next idle villager  ·  [ ] game speed  ·  command buttons show their hotkey

Touch — tap a unit to select, then tap a tree / enemy / ground to gather / attack / move  ·  drag to pan  ·  pinch to zoom  ·  ☷ button to box-select

Villagers gather 🪵 wood, 🍖 food and 🪙 gold and construct buildings.
Houses raise your population cap. Each Age unlocks stronger units and buildings.

Choose difficulty

Easy: the enemy plays defensively. Normal: balanced. Hard: aggressive early raids.
